03 - EDABK Lab

Respiratory Disease Classification

Research project on a hierarchical embedded system for diagnosing respiratory diseases from sound features. The paper was published in IEEE Access, vol. 13, 2025.

  • Five target classes: Healthy, COPD, URTI, Bronchitis, and Pneumonia.
  • Random Forest and ANN components integrated into an FPGA-oriented system.
  • Feature work spans recording-level and frame-level respiratory sound signals.
