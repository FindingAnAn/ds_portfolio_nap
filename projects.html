03 · Respiratory Disease Classification (IEEE Access 2025)

Hierarchical embedded system for classifying 5 respiratory diseases using FPGA-integrated Random Forest models — published IEEE Access 2025. 98.81% accuracy across Healthy, COPD, URTI, Bronchitis, and Pneumonia.

  • Recording-level features (Layer 1): RMSE, EED, Temporal Centroid, Log Attack Time, amplitude statistics.
  • Frame-level features (Layers 2 & 3): ZCR, mean, std, skewness, kurtosis over 20ms and 35ms overlapping frames.
  • 3-layer hierarchical architecture — 98.81% overall accuracy on FPGA platform.
