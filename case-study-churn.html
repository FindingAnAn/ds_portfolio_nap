S

Situation

Vietnam Post had no standardized early-warning process for B2B churn

Current process Customer care was mainly reactive

Teams often responded after activity had already fallen or stopped.

Decision gap No shared method to prioritize accounts

Historical behavior was not converted into a consistent risk ranking.

Business impact Retention opportunities could be missed

Late detection reduced the time available to contact and retain customers.

Operational constraint Customer-care capacity was limited

The team needed a short, explainable list rather than alerts for everyone.

ChurnNo items and no revenue during the next 2 months
ActiveAny item activity or positive revenue
