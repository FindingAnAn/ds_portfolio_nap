1. Business Problem

Vietnam Post's B2B delivery services faced invisible customer attrition — volume drops and churn that only became visible after significant revenue loss. The goal was to build a production ML system that detects at-risk customers before they churn and exports actionable, explainable risk lists to retention teams.

  • Targets: (1) binary churn in next quarter, (2) >50% volume decline vs 3-month average.
  • Users: AI Technology Department, customer retention, account management.
  • Decision: weekly top-10% at-risk customer list with SHAP-derived top-3 retention drivers per customer.
  • Data source: BCCP system — weekly transactional ZIP files ingested into PostgreSQL.

2. Data Engineering

Weekly ZIP files from BCCP are ingested, validated, and loaded into PostgreSQL through an automated pipeline:

Ingestion (DAG 1)
  • Scans ZIP files on weekly schedule
  • Schema validation + duplicate detection
  • Outlier handling (IQR / domain rules)
  • Clean records loaded into PostgreSQL
Feature Groups (10 groups)
  • Volume EWMA + delta-EWMA signals
  • Sliding windows: 3 / 6 / 12-month
  • Recency, frequency, monetary (RFM)
  • Service quality, complaints, tenure

3. 5-DAG Airflow Pipeline (Kubernetes)

DAG 1IngestZIP scan, schema validation, PostgreSQL load
DAG 2FeaturesSQL-native sliding-window pivot (3/6/12mo)
DAG 3Dataset7-step: scope → tier → EWMA → split → prototype → label → weight
DAG 4TrainXGBoost + walk-forward CV + guardrails
DAG 5ScoreTop-10% CSV + SHAP top-3 drivers

Each DAG is isolated, enabling parallel EDA and data quality checks without blocking the scoring pipeline.

4. Modeling & Validation

Walk-forward expanding-window validation was used to select the optimal training window size by comparing AUC across folds against a Logistic Regression baseline. StandardScaler was fitted strictly on the training fold to prevent leakage.

Sample-weighted loss was applied to address class imbalance. Prototype selection and semi-supervised labeling were applied in the dataset preparation DAG before model training.

5. Scoring & Explainability

All active customers are scored weekly. The top 10% at-risk (by churn score or >50% volume decline vs 3-month average) are exported to a CSV with SHAP gain-based top-3 risk drivers per customer.
