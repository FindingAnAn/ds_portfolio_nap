1. Business Goal

The project supports customer-care teams by identifying active B2B customers who may stop using services in the next period. Instead of producing only a model score, the workflow creates a practical output: a short list of high-risk customers and the main signals behind each risk score.

2. Data Pipeline

Monthly source files are loaded into PostgreSQL, checked for basic quality, and transformed into customer-level features. The features combine long-term customer history with recent behavior, such as recency, inactive months, revenue change, and order activity.

Input
  • Customer profile data.
  • Monthly transaction data.
  • Historical activity windows.
Feature Logic
  • Lifetime value and activity.
  • Recent decline or inactivity.
  • Time-aware training windows.

3. Modeling Approach

The model is trained with time-aware validation so evaluation follows the monthly nature of the business problem. XGBoost is used for churn classification, and SHAP is used to explain the main drivers for each high-risk customer.

4. Final Output

The final deliverable is a monthly risk table that can be used by business teams. Each row contains customer ID, churn probability, risk tier, and explanation signals. This keeps the model tied to a clear operational decision instead of remaining a standalone experiment.
