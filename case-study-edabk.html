S

Situation

Respiratory screening relied heavily on specialist auscultation

Current process Doctors manually interpreted lung sounds

Screening depended on clinical experience and careful listening.

Access gap Specialists were not always available

Resource-limited settings needed accessible diagnostic support.

Clinical challenge Diseases share similar acoustic signs

Crackles and wheezes appear at different temporal resolutions.

Deployment need Point-of-care use required local inference

The system needed efficient operation without cloud dependency.

Clinical roleAssist screening and diagnostic review
TargetHealthy, COPD, URTI, Bronchitis, Pneumonia

We combined multi-resolution audio analysis with embedded inference
