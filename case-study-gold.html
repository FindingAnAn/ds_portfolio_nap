P

Problem

Gold forecasting required point-in-time data and a fair multi-horizon benchmark

Data boundary Signals arrive at different frequencies and release times

Prices, yields, volatility, policy risk, oil, and CFTC positions could not be joined by date alone.

Model uncertainty Complex sequence models do not automatically perform best

The project needed simple controls and tabular challengers beside deep time-series models.

Horizon risk Recursive forecasts can accumulate error

Each future session needed an independent target and estimator rather than feeding predictions back into the model.

Evidence need Every result had to remain reproducible

Data hashes, metrics, models, forecasts, resource use, and later realized Opens needed one run identity.

Input contractInformation known after the latest completed gold session
Target contractGold Open for each of the next 10 sessions

Market regimesLeakage-safe analogs based on historical momentum and volatility
Controlled run146 direct-model features; sequence inputs used 1 or 15 variables by architecture
Target isolationFuture labels stay outside the master feature table and future-name prefixes are blocked.
Horizon purgeHorizon h trains only through cutoff − h, with a 10-session purge boundary.
Release timingCFTC joins after report availability; monthly FRED fields without vintage timestamps are excluded.
Historical transformsNo backward-fill; seasonal analogs must be at least one year before the forecast date.

Evidence And Limits

Architecture claims are separated from performance claims

Verified in repository
  • Five ingestion adapters and four PostgreSQL schemas
  • Eleven SQL stages with target isolation
  • Nine benchmark candidates across sequence and tabular families
  • Completed run with 146 features and six rolling origins
  • RMSE 87.07 and MAPE 1.66% for the selected blend
Current limits
  • Only six forecast origins; the performance estimate remains uncertain
  • Daily FRED history still has vintage and revision risk without ALFRED
  • Forecast dates use a US business-day estimate, not the official CME calendar
  • Intervals are empirical and evidence signals are rule-based, not causal
  • Research pipeline, not a trading strategy or financial advice
