S

Situation

Gold forecasting required a unified view of fragmented market signals

Current process Analysis depended on separate data sources

Gold, USD, equities, rates, inflation, and oil were collected independently.

Decision gap No repeatable forecasting workflow

Manual analysis made experiments difficult to reproduce and compare.

Market reality Gold responds to cross-market conditions

A price-only dataset could miss macroeconomic and risk signals.

Project need One auditable experiment pipeline

The project needed consistent data, features, validation, and prediction outputs.

Input contractInformation available on or before the forecast date
Target contractGold price on the next available trading day

Evidence And Limits

Architecture claims are separated from performance claims

Verified in repository

Current limits
  • Research pipeline, not trading advice
  • No transaction-cost or strategy backtest
  • No automated retraining scheduler
  • Model must be retrained after feature-schema changes
